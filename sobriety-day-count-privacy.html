Your recovery data is private by design

Sober Count: Alcohol, Nicotine ("Sober Count", "the app") is provided by CSSMatter. The counter, focuses, start dates, check-ins, moods, cravings, reasons, notes, savings settings, favorites, app-lock settings and support contacts are designed to stay on your device. They are not uploaded to our community database or sent to RevenueCat.

Important: Sober Count is a self-management tool, not a medical service, diagnostic tool, treatment provider or emergency service.

Information we process

Data stored locally on your device

  • Your name, optional date of birth, goals and app language.
  • Focuses, sobriety start dates, check-ins, lapse history, moods, cravings, journal-style reflections and savings estimates.
  • Favorite quotes, theme, alternate icon, widget and notification preferences.
  • Optional support-person details, protected using device storage where supported.

Local data remains on your device unless you export it or include it in a device backup managed by Apple or Google.

Account and authentication data

If you create an account or use Google or Apple sign-in, Firebase Authentication processes your account identifier, email address, provider information and verification state. Apple may provide a private relay email. Your password is handled by Firebase and is not visible to CSSMatter.

Community data

If you join the optional community, Cloud Firestore stores your public username, text posts, comments, emoji reactions, moderation metadata and timestamps. Never post medical records, contact details or other sensitive information. Other users can see community content until it is removed.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes payment information. RevenueCat receives an app user identifier, product, entitlement and transaction status to unlock Premium. We do not receive full card details. Private recovery notes and counter data are not sent to RevenueCat.

Notifications and widgets

Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device. Widget content is generated on your device. The app lock does not hide information you choose to show in a home-screen widget.

How information is used

  • Operate the counter, personalization, account and community features.
  • Verify purchases, restore entitlements and prevent purchase fraud.
  • Protect the community and enforce guidelines.
  • Respond to support, privacy and legal requests.
  • Maintain security, diagnose errors and comply with law.

We do not sell personal information, run third-party advertising, or use private recovery data for targeted advertising.

Service providers and international processing

  • Google Firebase — authentication and optional community database.
  • RevenueCat — subscriptions, lifetime purchases and entitlement status.
  • Apple and Google — sign-in, app distribution and payments.
  • Expo — application build and delivery infrastructure.

These providers may process data in countries different from yours under their own legal safeguards.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local data remains until you delete it in the app, clear app data or uninstall the app. Account and community records are retained while your account is active and as reasonably needed for security, dispute resolution or legal compliance. Deleted community content may remain briefly in backups or security logs.

You can export local data, remove individual posts, use the app without an account, or request deletion. See the Account Deletion page. Purchase records may be retained by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as required for financial, fraud-prevention and legal purposes.

Security, children and changes

We use platform security, encrypted transport and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so do not place highly sensitive medical or identifying information in community posts.

The app is not directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum digital-consent age required in their country. We do not knowingly collect a child’s account data without legally valid consent.

We may update this policy when features, providers or laws change. The date above identifies the latest version.
