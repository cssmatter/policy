Account and data deletion request

PrivacySupport

Before you request deletion

  • Export anything you want to keep from Profile → Export my data.
  • Delete community posts you want removed immediately from Community → My posts.
  • Account deletion does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Cancel it in your store settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes local app data from that device but does not delete an online account.

Request permanent deletion

Send an email from the address used for your Sober Count account. Use the subject “Delete Sober Count account”. We may send a verification message to protect your account. After verification, we will delete or anonymize the Firebase account, public profile and community content associated with it, except records we must retain for fraud prevention, transaction evidence, security or law.

Request deletion

Target completion: within 30 days after identity verification. We will confirm when the request is completed.

What is deleted or retained

Deleted or anonymized: Firebase sign-in account, public username, community posts, comments and reactions linked to the account, subject to backup delay and legal requirements.

On-device data: remove it in the app or uninstall the app. We cannot remotely erase information stored only on your device.

Store transactions: Apple, Google and RevenueCat may retain purchase records required to restore purchases, prevent fraud or meet legal obligations. These providers control their own retention.
