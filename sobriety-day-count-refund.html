Payments are handled by your app store

Apple App Store or Google Play processes all Sober Count purchases. CSSMatter does not receive your complete payment-card details and normally cannot directly approve, issue or track a store refund.

Refund eligibility and timing are decided by Apple or Google under their policies and the consumer law that applies to you.

Request a refund

Request from AppleRequest from Google Play

Sign in with the store account used for the purchase and select the Sober Count transaction.

Subscriptions and cancellation

Cancelling prevents future renewal but generally does not refund the current billing period. Premium normally remains available until the paid period ends.

  • iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sober Count.
  • Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Sober Count.

A lifetime purchase is a one-time, non-renewing purchase. Any refund request still goes to the store that processed it.

Duplicate, accidental or unrecognized charges

Use the store links above promptly. If Premium did not unlock after a successful charge, first use “Restore purchases” in the app while signed in to the same app and store accounts. For technical help, email cssmatter@gmail.com with the platform, approximate purchase date and store order number. Do not send card numbers, passwords or identity documents.
