Purchase help

  1. Confirm you are signed in to the same Sober Count account and Apple/Google store account used to purchase.
  2. Open Profile → Sob Premium → Restore purchases.
  3. Use Refresh purchase status.
  4. If access is still missing, contact support with the store order number.

For money-back requests, see the Refund Policy.

Private tracking and community

Your counter and private recovery entries work offline. Community posting requires a verified account and an internet connection. You can delete your own posts from My Posts. App lock and widgets are device features and may require a signed development or store build rather than Expo Go.

Urgent or medical help

Sober Count is not monitored and cannot provide emergency, crisis or medical care. If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. For withdrawal, detoxification, medication or treatment questions, contact a qualified medical professional.
